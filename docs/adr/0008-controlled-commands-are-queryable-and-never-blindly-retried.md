# Controlled commands are queryable and never blindly retried

After a disconnect, an R1 client may know that command bytes were written without knowing whether the daemon applied them. Every releasable Follow-up or Stop therefore has a stable commandId, durable server-side deduplication, a result query, and a minimal secure-storage receipt containing no prompt or transcript payload. On restart, any unresolved receipt is reconciled before the client permits another Controlled command. An indeterminate command enters `UNKNOWN` and is never blindly retried. Without secure Creation storage, read-only distribution may remain session-only, but Follow-up and Stop do not ship; if Paseo cannot provide deduplication and result queries, private transport tests may continue but Controlled Actions do not ship.
